    Specialised Service

    Covering Your Insurance Gaps —
    Insurance Audit

    An insurance audit is like a health check for your policies. We review all your existing coverage — annual premiums, coverage limits, cross-product benefits, and gaps — against your current risk profile. Our only objective is to bring you the right solution that maps to your present risk tolerance.

    Many businesses are either over-insured in areas they don't need or dangerously under-covered where it matters. An Insugo audit ensures every rupee of premium works hard for you.
